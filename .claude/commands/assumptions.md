description: Audit assumptions before proceeding, especially after repeated failures
---
Before proceeding, list every assumption you're making about this problem — what you believe to be true but haven't directly verified in this session. For each one, mark it as VERIFIED (you checked it directly — schema, logs, docs, actual behavior) or ASSUMED (you're inferring or guessing). For anything marked ASSUMED, state how you could verify it, and verify the highest-risk ones before proposing a fix.
